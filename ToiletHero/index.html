Our technology

Lifetime warranty

We offer a lifetime warranty for all of our products to let you always be able to repair instead of replace.

SmartFlush™ technology

We designed a new technology called SmartFlush™ to allow for an automatic adjust of water flow depending on the waste, while reducing the needed water by 40% compared to others toilets.

NanoShield coating

With research we were able to put nanoparticule into our products to make a self cleaning and anti-bacterial surfaces to prevent stains and buildup. In partneurship with Swirl&Shine.

HeroConnect app

Thanks to our design and tight integration with smart technologies we made an mobile application letting you in controls of your toilet at each moment. You can get maintenance alerts, get statistics to improve toilet use, or even see the remains of toilet paper.
